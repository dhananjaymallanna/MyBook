## How to use these prompts

Start by clarifying users, scope, and constraints. Convert the prompt into functional requirements (FR) and non-functional requirements (NFR), agree on measurable targets, then explore architecture, failure modes, and trade-offs. Each prompt retains its original details and adds a practice checklist; none supplies a worked solution.
